---
title: Board
description: Organize work in default or custom lanes, reorder cards and lanes, and resize the desktop issue panel.
---

The board contains work explicitly added from a project's issue list. New boards start with **Todo**, **In progress**, and **Done**, with no cards. Board lanes and placements are saved per project and shared with your team.

## Add work

1. Open a project and select **Board**.
2. Select **Add issues**.
3. Search or select issues, choose a destination lane, then select **Add to board**.

**Create issue** captures Markdown content, including tags, in the project list. It does not automatically add the issue to the board. Use **Add issues** when it is ready for work. Only open issues are offered; reopen a closed issue to plan it again.

Use the card's kebab menu beside its drag handle to remove only its board placement. The issue, comments, and attachments remain available in **List** and can be added back later.

## Manage lanes

Select **Manage lanes** to:

- Change a lane's position with its **Move up** and **Move down** buttons. The list order is the board's left-to-right order.
- Remove a visible lane using its remove button.
- Restore a removed lane under **Add an existing lane**.
- Enter a name under **Create a new lane**, then select **Create lane** (or press Enter).

Select **Save board** to apply your changes, or **Cancel** to discard them. Keep at least one visible lane. Custom names must be unique, contain 1–60 characters, and differ from the built-in names; each project supports up to 30 custom lanes.

<Note>
  Removing a lane hides it without deleting its issues or placements. Add it back to see those cards again. The board summary shows how many cards are in hidden lanes.
</Note>

Wide boards scroll horizontally on desktop rather than squeezing the cards.

## Reorder lanes

Drag a lane by its heading and drop it on another lane to take that lane's place; a highlighted edge shows where it will land. Its cards move with it. The new order saves immediately and is shared with your team. If saving fails, the board shows an error and returns to the previous order.

To reorder with a keyboard or on a touch screen, use the move buttons in **Manage lanes**, then select **Save board**. Restored and newly created lanes are added at the end, where you can move them.

## Move work

Drag a card by its handle before or after another card, in the same lane or another lane. A highlighted line shows where it will land. Drop in the lane's empty space to put it at the end. Only the handle starts a drag; dragging a lane heading still moves the whole lane.

For keyboard or mobile access, open a card's kebab menu and use **Move up**, **Move down**, **Move to top**, or **Move to bottom**. These controls also work on a same-lane selection. Choosing a different lane from the menu appends the cards there.

Moves save immediately, persist across reloads, and are shared with your team. They do not change issue content, state, tags, timestamps, or unsaved edits.

The permanent selection bar combines the board/lane counts, **Select visible issues**, and bulk actions. Select individual cards with their checkboxes, Cmd/Ctrl-click anywhere on a card to toggle selection, or Shift-click to select a range. Ordinary clicks on the card body, padding, or metadata open issue details; checkboxes and the kebab menu keep their own actions. Then use the selection bar or a selected card's menu to move or remove the selection. Dragging a selected card’s handle moves the whole selection. Failed operations remain selected for retry; successful operations are kept. Changing search or hiding lanes removes hidden cards from the selection. Removing cards never deletes their issues.

Selected cards are kept in their displayed order when dragged as a group. Search filters do not remove hidden cards or scramble their relative order: drops are positioned relative to the visible target in the complete lane. Menu moves use the full lane order, including search-hidden cards. Failed reorders leave the saved order and selection intact for retry.

New cards and cards moved from another lane using **Send to board** are appended; sending a card to its existing lane keeps its position. Existing boards retain their prior issue-number order on upgrade, until you rearrange them. Only the built-in **Done** lane counts as completed in project progress.

Closing an issue does not move its card. A closed card keeps its lane, shows a **Closed** tag, and no longer counts as unfinished work. Move it to **Done** or remove it from the board when you no longer want to see it.

The board of an [archived project](/guides/projects#archive-a-project) is read-only: cards, lanes, and **Manage lanes** cannot be changed until the project is restored.

## Read and edit an issue

Select a card body to open its issue and discussion. Mentions and tags stay in the content rather than a separate properties card or tag list. On desktop, this opens a panel alongside the board or list without losing your search.

- Drag the panel's left divider to resize it. Your browser remembers the width across visits.
- Focus the divider and use Left/Right arrows to adjust it; Shift makes larger steps. Home/End select its size limits.
- Double-click the divider to reset to the default width.
- Use **Open issue in full page** for a dedicated page, or close the panel to return to the collection.

The panel narrows automatically on smaller desktop screens while retaining your preferred width. On mobile, issues open in a full page instead.

## Find a card

**Search issues** matches titles, issue numbers, and tags, case-insensitively. Clear the search to see all cards in visible lanes. If work is missing, check **Manage lanes** for removed lanes, **Add issues** for unplaced work, or open **List** to see every open or closed project issue.

All authenticated workspace members can collaborate on boards. For issue content and discussion, see [issues](/guides/issues).
